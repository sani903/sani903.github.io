Sanidhya Vijayvargiya

Research Engineer · Samsung Research America

I am a Research Engineer at Samsung Research America, where I work on reliable and capable AI agents, with a particular focus on small language models in resource-constrained settings.

I received my M.S. in Intelligent Information Systems from the Language Technologies Institute at Carnegie Mellon University in 2025, where I was advised by Prof. Graham Neubig. Before that, I completed my B.E. in Computer Science at BITS Pilani in 2024.

Research interests

My research investigates how AI systems can learn underlying capabilities that generalize across tasks and environments. I am particularly interested in informational reasoning: how agents recognize what information a task requires and use that as guidance for their actions.

Modeling informational state
Understanding how models represent what a task requires, what information they possess, and whether it is sufficient to act.
Strategic information acquisition and management
Learning how agents acquire, retain, and update information through tools, memory, and interaction.
